Academic Projects

  • Transformer Protection System Simulation - MATLAB/Simulink:

    Designed and simulated transformer protection scheme incorporating overcurrent relay logic; analyzed fault scenarios (short-circuit/overload) and evaluated relay coordination and fault isolation response.

  • Solar PV System Modeling & Performance Evaluation - MATLAB/Simulink:

    Developed dynamic PV model to generate I-V and P-V characteristics under varying irradiance; assessed output efficiency and system performance behavior.

  • Induction Motor Performance Analysis - MATLAB:

    Modeled torque–speed and slip characteristics of induction motor; analyzed load variation effects and starting current behavior for industrial application study.
